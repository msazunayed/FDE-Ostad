02 Operating model

Not staff augmentation. A deployment unit.

FDE teams combine product judgment, platform engineering, and field context. The work starts where your process already runs and ends with your team able to operate it.

01

Diagnose in the field

Map the real workflow, data contracts, failure modes, and decision points before committing to an architecture.

02

Build inside the workflow

Ship a working system into the operating environment early, then harden it against real usage and edge cases.

03

Transfer capability

Document, train, pair, and leave behind an internal team that can extend the system without a permanent vendor dependency.

03 Capabilities

Built for the space between strategy and production.

Each engagement is scoped around an operational outcome, not a slide deck. The team stays close to the constraint until the system survives real work.

A

AI workflow systems

Design and deploy copilots, decision support, retrieval systems, and automation layers that fit existing approval and escalation paths.

  • Workflow instrumentation
  • Human-in-the-loop controls
  • Evaluation harnesses
B

Data product engineering

Turn brittle pipelines and scattered knowledge into usable data products with clear ownership.

C

Internal tools

Build operator-facing interfaces that reduce swivel-chair work and make complex systems legible.

D

Integration hardening

Connect identity, data, legacy systems, and third-party APIs without creating a fragile prototype.

E

Enablement and handoff

Rituals, docs, runbooks, and pairing plans that make the system operable after the deployment team leaves.
